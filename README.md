# 3D Exotic Garden Games

A cozy 3D exotic-plant growing and selling game.

## Build 0.1 gameplay loop
1. Walk to the outdoor seed shop and claim the first Venus flytrap seed free.
2. Enter the greenhouse and use one of 2 starting grow spots.
3. Plant and water the seed through visible growth stages.
4. Harvest the mature Venus flytrap.
5. Take it to the potting and sales hut.
6. Pot the plant and sell it to a customer.
7. Use the money to expand the greenhouse.

## Greenhouse progression
Capacity tiers: **2 → 5 → 10 → 20 → 35 → 50 plants**.

## First prototype
The first prototype is browser-playable 3D using Three.js. It is designed as a fast vertical slice before moving into higher-detail models, animations, more exotic plants, genetics, mutations, insects, climate systems, and expanded customer behavior.
